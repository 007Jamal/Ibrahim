ABOUT

I sit between technical understanding and careful communication.

What Sets My Work Apart

AI quality work is not only about spotting wrong answers. It involves understanding the task, checking evidence, recognizing unsupported inference, comparing alternatives, and writing a rationale another reviewer can defend. My software engineering and cybersecurity background gives me a practical foundation for working with technical material rather than evaluating it only at the surface.

“I want the reasoning behind a quality judgment to be as clear as the judgment itself.”

That same principle carries into documentation, annotation, research, creative writing, and administrative work: understand the objective, inspect the details, and leave behind something another person can use.

HOW I WORK

A practical workflow for AI evaluation, documentation and data quality tasks.

01
Understand the taskIdentify the outcome, constraints, audience, evidence requirements and evaluation criteria.
02
Inspect the evidenceSeparate what is supported from what is plausible, inferred, incomplete or contradicted.
03
Record the reasonWrite concise rationales that point to the exact response, field, label or instruction.
04
Improve the processUse repeated errors and disagreements to identify unclear guidelines or workflow improvements.
The goal is not to make every output sound impressive. The goal is to make the quality of the work inspectable.
